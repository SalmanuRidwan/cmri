About the programme

Awareness Campaign on National Responsibility

The Awareness Campaign on National Responsibility is a civic education and public awareness programme designed to encourage Nigerians to understand and practise their responsibilities as citizens.

Nation-building is not the responsibility of government alone. Citizens, communities, businesses, institutions and leaders all have a role to play in building a more responsible, peaceful and prosperous society.

Every citizen has a role. Every action matters.

At a glance

Our Focus

The campaign raises public awareness around:

Responsible citizenship

Understanding and living out our duties.

Respect for laws & institutions

Honouring the rules that hold society together.

Integrity & honesty

Doing right, even when no one is watching.

Community service

Giving time and effort to the people around us.

Public property

Protecting what belongs to everyone.

Environmental responsibility

Caring for the places we share.

Peaceful coexistence

Respect, tolerance and unity.

Accountability & participation

Responsible relationships between citizens and institutions.

Objectives

Key Objectives

  • Educate: Help citizens understand their rights, duties and responsibilities.
  • Create awareness: Promote positive national values and responsible behaviour.
  • Inspire action: Encourage positive contribution in homes, communities and workplaces.
  • Promote unity: Strengthen respect, tolerance, peaceful coexistence and shared responsibility.
  • Encourage accountability: Promote responsible relationships between citizens, communities and institutions.

Our Approach

Accessible, community-focused channels: community sensitisation, schools and youth engagements, public dialogues, radio and media campaigns, social media, street interviews, educational materials and community outreach. Our goal is to make national responsibility a practical everyday behaviour, not just an idea discussed at civic events.
