About the programme

Voice of the Citizens Podcast

Voice of the Citizens is CMRI’s podcast: a space for real conversations, honest street interviews and practical ideas on how ordinary Nigerians can help build a better country.

Hosted by Aisha Nafiu (Haj. Ummi), each episode puts citizenship, mindset reform, youth leadership and national responsibility at the centre of the conversation.

Real conversations. Real solutions. Better Nigeria.

Discussion Highlights

Themes that run through the series.

Citizenship & National Responsibility

What every Nigerian owes the country, and each other.

Mindset Reform for National Development

Changing how we think so that change can last.

Youth Leadership & Patriotism

Character, service and leadership among young people.

Building the Nigeria We Desire

Practical steps from citizens, not just government.

The Role of Citizens in Nation Building

Everyday action that adds up to national progress.
