About the programme

Nasarawa Citizens Future Dialogue

The Nasarawa Citizens Future Dialogue (NCFD 2026) is a high-impact civic engagement platform that brings citizens and key stakeholders together to discuss the realities, challenges, opportunities and aspirations shaping the future of Nasarawa State.

It creates a structured space for young people, civil society organisations, women, entrepreneurs, community leaders, professionals, policymakers, researchers and the media to exchange perspectives and explore practical solutions.

Citizens should not only be part of development outcomes. They should be part of conversations about the future they want to build.

At a glance

The NCFD 2026 Conversation

Five tracks shape the Dialogue.

Economic Reality, Livelihoods & Opportunity

Employment, cost of living, entrepreneurship, agriculture, markets, finance and youth livelihoods.

Governance, Public Trust & Service Delivery

Citizen expectations, accountability, public services and community participation.

Youth, Education & the Future of Nasarawa

Skills, education, leadership, employment, innovation and youth opportunities.

Community Development & Social Cohesion

Community priorities, inclusion, peaceful coexistence and local development.

Citizens’ Solutions & Policy Recommendations

Participants propose practical solutions and development priorities.

Objectives

Our Objectives

  • Amplify citizen voice: A structured platform to express experiences, priorities and expectations.
  • Document key challenges: Capture emerging socio-economic and community realities across the state.
  • Promote civic participation: Encourage constructive engagement and responsible citizenship.
  • Connect stakeholders: Bring citizens, communities, business, civil society and institutions into dialogue.
  • Generate recommendations: Translate citizen perspectives into documented priorities and actionable ideas.

Why NCFD?

Nasarawa is experiencing changing economic, social and demographic realities. Young people are seeking education, skills, employment and opportunities. Businesses and farmers are navigating changing markets, and communities are responding to development, social and livelihood challenges. NCFD brings these experiences together.

Five-year vision

Five-year vision

01

Year 1 · Establish

Launch NCFD and document the priorities of Nasarawa citizens.

02

Year 2 · Deepen

Expand community participation, research and stakeholder engagement.

03

Year 3 · Connect

Strengthen partnerships with civil society, business, academia and development organisations.

04

Year 4 · Scale

Expand community dialogues and citizen engagement across the state.

05

Year 5 · Sustain

Establish NCFD as a recurring civic platform for continuous dialogue and documentation.

Beyond Nasarawa: the NCFD model is designed to be adaptable to other states and regions. Lessons from Nasarawa can feed a broader network of Citizens Future Dialogues across Nigeria, with the long-term possibility of a national citizen dialogue platform.
