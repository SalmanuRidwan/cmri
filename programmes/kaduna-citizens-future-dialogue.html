About the programme

Kaduna Citizens Future Dialogue

The Kaduna Citizens Future Dialogue (KCFD) 2026 brings citizens and key stakeholders together to discuss the realities, challenges, expectations and opportunities shaping the future of Kaduna State.

It creates space for young people, civil society organisations, entrepreneurs, women, community leaders, professionals, media, researchers and governance stakeholders to share experiences, exchange ideas and develop practical recommendations.

A conversation about the Kaduna we experience today, and the Kaduna we want to build tomorrow.

At a glance

Key Conversation Areas

Four themes anchor the Dialogue.

Economic Reality & Youth Opportunities

Employment, cost of living, entrepreneurship, skills, income and access to opportunity.

Governance & Public Trust

Citizen expectations, public accountability, service delivery and civic participation.

Youth & Kaduna’s Future

Education, leadership, skills, innovation, employment and young people’s aspirations.

Citizens’ Solutions

Community ideas, development priorities and practical recommendations from participants.

Objectives

What We Seek to Achieve

  • Participation: Create space for meaningful citizen participation.
  • Documentation: Document Kaduna citizens’ socio-economic realities and priorities.
  • Civic awareness: Strengthen civic awareness and responsible citizenship.
  • Dialogue: Encourage constructive dialogue between citizens and stakeholders.
  • Solutions: Identify practical community and policy solutions and produce evidence for future programmes.

Key Output: Kaduna Citizens Expectations Report 2026

Insights from the Dialogue will be documented in a flagship report covering citizens’ socio-economic priorities, youth expectations, community concerns, governance and service-delivery perspectives, and practical recommendations, a reference for civil society, researchers, media, development organisations and the private sector.
