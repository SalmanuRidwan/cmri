Who we are

A nation rises when its citizens do.

CMRI works to promote responsible citizenship, restore national values and inspire behavioural change through strategic programmes, advocacy and community engagement.

Our Vision

To rebuild Nigeria’s moral, civic and ethical foundations by restoring national values and shaping a new civic mindset rooted in integrity, discipline and responsibility.

Our Mission

To design and implement programmes that educate, enlighten and empower Nigerians to embrace the core values necessary for national development, unity and prosperity.

Core focus areas

What we do

A participatory, evidence-based approach combining education, engagement and advocacy to drive lasting mindset and behavioural change.

01Civic Education & Value Reorientation
02Youth Leadership & Character Development
03Community Engagement & Social Behaviour Change
04Public Awareness Campaigns & Advocacy
05Research on National Values & Social Transformation
  • Promote moral, civic and ethical education among Nigerians.
  • Run national orientation programmes on discipline, responsibility, honesty, empathy and respect.
  • Address corruption, indiscipline, intolerance and ignorance.
  • Collaborate with schools, communities, government and private organisations.
  • Develop training manuals, workshops, outreaches and media campaigns.
  • Mentor youth on leadership, character and national responsibility.
  • Advocate for policies that strengthen national values.
  • Mobilise citizens for nation-building through voluntary service.
  • Support research on social behaviour and cultural transformation.
  • Operate transparently and ethically for public benefit.
